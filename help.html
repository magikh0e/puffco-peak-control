changelog

Peak Pro Control runs a Puffco Peak Pro from a browser tab over Bluetooth, with no app to install and no account. This page covers what you need, how to connect the first time, what each part of the app does, and what to do when a connection fails. For heat settings by dab style, see the dab temperature guide.

Before you start

  • Chrome or Edge. On a computer, or Chrome on an Android phone. iPhone, iPad, Safari and Firefox don't support Web Bluetooth, so the page can't connect from them. It has been tested with Chrome on Windows 11; other systems should work but haven't been tried yet.
  • A Peak Pro on current firmware. Firmware older than AB uses a different Bluetooth layout, and the app tells you if it finds one. Update it with the Puffco app first.
  • One connection at a time. The Peak Pro talks to one thing at once. Close the Puffco app on your phone, or turn off the phone's Bluetooth, before you connect.
  • Close by. It works within Bluetooth range of the device. It isn't remote control over the internet.
  • A paired computer. The Peak Pro only answers over an encrypted link, so your computer has to pair with it once. The next section covers that.

Connect for the first time

  1. Close the Puffco app on your phone.
  2. Put the Peak Pro in pairing mode: hold the button until the logo glows blue. It turns off and back on before the blue light appears.
  3. Open the app, press Connect, pick the Peak Pro in Chrome's list, and press Pair.
  4. The line under the device name shows each step: Connecting, Pairing, Unlocking. While it says Pairing (… s left), accept any prompt from Chrome or Windows. It waits up to 30 seconds.
  5. When it says Connected, the ring shows the live temperature and the tabs appear below it.

After the first time, Connect works without pairing mode. Pairing a computer may mean the Puffco app on your phone has to pair again the next time you use it.

Pairing on Windows

Windows Settings may not list the Peak Pro at all. If the app says this computer isn't paired, the pairing didn't happen at the system level. In testing, what worked was Windows' confirm-only custom pairing; the README's pairing section has the details. If Windows keeps saying a pairing is already in progress, restart the computer and try again.

The app, part by part

The ring

The big number is the heater's live temperature. Under it, the device's state: Idle, Heating to 545°F, Ready, Cooling down or Asleep. During a heat cycle the ring fills as each stage runs, and it glows in the light color of the profile in use.

Heating

Pick one of the four profiles under Heat profile, then press Heat with and the profile's name. While it heats:

  • Stop ends the heat cycle.
  • +10°F (or +5°C) raises the target, and +10 s adds time.
  • Target, Preheat or Time left, Battery and Chamber show below.

The profile buttons are locked until the heat cycle ends.

Profiles tab

Edit each of the four profiles and press Save to write it to the device. The device's limits apply:

  • Name: up to 31 characters.
  • Temperature: 400–644°F (204–340°C).
  • Duration: 15–120 seconds.
  • Boost: adds 1–36°F (1–20°C) for 1–60 seconds.
  • Light color: if a profile uses one of the device's animated lights, the field says so.

Copy to My profiles keeps a copy on the Library tab.

Library tab

  • Templates: 12 ready-made profiles and 4 sets of four, with temperatures from the Cape Cod Cannabis and Leafly dabbing guides. Star one to add it to Favorites.
  • Build your own set of four: pick a lowest and highest temperature and it spreads four profiles between them.
  • My profiles: profiles saved in this browser, with Back up and Restore.

Loading puts a profile into one of the four slots. The profile it replaces is kept in My profiles, unless it matches a template. Loading is off while the device heats.

Device tab

  • Device name: up to 29 characters.
  • Light brightness: 15–100%.
  • Stealth mode: dims the lights while heating.
  • Ready mode: picks which profile the device's ready mode uses, or turns it off.
  • Mood light: turns the light on in any color for two hours, or off.
  • Turn off device: asks first. Press the device's button to wake it again.

A setting this firmware doesn't have is greyed out.

Stats tab

Total dabs, dabs per day, dabs left on this charge, a trip counter, total heating time, battery and charging details, the chamber, and device details such as the firmware build. Refresh reads them again.

Protocol log tab

Every Bluetooth exchange with the device. Show polling reads adds the regular status reads. Copy log copies it for a bug report.

°F and °C

The switch at the top right changes every temperature in the app, including the profile editor and the templates. The browser remembers your choice.

When it won't connect

When a connection fails, the app shows what went wrong and what to try. Here are the same messages with a little more detail.

The Peak Pro isn't in Chrome's list
  • Wake the device with its button. Asleep, it doesn't show up.
  • Close the Puffco app on your phone. While the phone is connected, the device doesn't show up anywhere else.
  • Move closer, and check that Bluetooth is on for the computer.
  • Connecting to this computer for the first time? Put it in pairing mode (hold the button until the logo glows blue) and press Connect again.
The app says:The Peak Pro refused the connection

A device that hasn't paired with this computer only accepts a new connection while it is in pairing mode.

  1. Close the Puffco app on your phone, or turn off Bluetooth on the phone.
  2. Hold the device button until the logo glows blue.
  3. Press Connect and pick the device again while it is glowing blue.
  4. If Windows asks to pair the device, allow it.
  5. Still failing? Remove the Peak Pro under Windows Settings > Bluetooth & devices, then try again.
The app says:This computer isn't paired with the Peak Pro, so it won't answer

Chrome connected, but the device ignores everything until the link is encrypted, and that needs a pairing at the system level.

  1. Close the app's tab and the Puffco app on your phone.
  2. Put the device in pairing mode (hold the button until the logo glows blue).
  3. Pair it with this computer from your system's Bluetooth settings. On Windows, see Pairing on Windows above.
  4. If Windows reports a pairing already in progress, restart the computer and pair again.
  5. Once it is paired, come back and press Connect.
The app says:The Peak Pro dropped the connection during setup
  1. Wait a few seconds and press Connect again.
  2. If it keeps dropping, put the device in pairing mode and connect again.
  3. Still stuck? Remove the Peak Pro under Windows Settings > Bluetooth & devices, then pair again.
The app says:The Peak Pro connected but didn't answer
  1. Remove the Peak Pro under Windows Settings > Bluetooth & devices if it is listed.
  2. Put the device in pairing mode and press Connect.
  3. Accept any pairing prompt from Chrome or Windows.
  4. Still stuck? Press Copy technical details under the message and report it.
The app says:This Peak Pro is on older firmware

Firmware older than AB uses a different Bluetooth layout that this app doesn't support. Update the device with the Puffco app, then connect again.

The app says:This browser can't use Bluetooth

Open the page in Chrome or Edge on a computer, or Chrome on an Android phone. If you're already in one of those, check that the computer or phone has Bluetooth turned on.

Connected, but a button or setting is greyed out
  • Loading templates and profiles is off while the device heats. Stop the heat cycle, or wait for it to end.
  • A setting the device's firmware doesn't have is greyed out on the Device tab.

Terpenes and temperature

Terpenes carry a concentrate's flavor and smell, and the lighter ones boil off first. Five of them make up most of what you taste in cannabis:

The big five terpenes, from lowest boiling point to highest
TerpeneBoils atAromaReported effects
Pinene311°F (155°C)Fresh pine, herbsAlertness, focus, memory
Myrcene334°F (168°C)Earthy, musky, ripe mangoRelaxing and sedating
Limonene349°F (176°C)Citrus, lemon and orange rindUplifting, energizing, stress relief
Linalool388°F (198°C)Floral, lavenderCalming, eases anxiety
Caryophyllene495°F (257°C)Black pepper, spice, woodCalming; the one terpene that also binds to CB2 receptors

Four of the five boil below the Peak Pro's lowest setting of 400°F (204°C), so every heat setting vaporizes them. The cooler the setting, the more of their flavor reaches you before it scorches. That's why the flavor templates sit at the low end and the cloud-chasing ones at the top.

Caryophyllene, the peppery one, is a heavier molecule and boils around 495°F (257°C), inside the Peak Pro's range. Many cannabis charts list it at 266°F (130°C), but that's its boiling point close to a vacuum, not at normal air pressure (PubChem).

Terpenes also evaporate slowly at room temperature, so keep concentrates sealed, cool and out of the light.

The boiling points match the dab temperature guide, which also lists THC, CBD and more terpenes. For more on what each terpene does and how growers preserve them, read Tropical Roots Maui's guide to the big five terpenes.

FAQ

Can I try it without a Peak Pro?

Yes. Open the demo (?demo on the address) and press Connect demo. It runs the real app against a simulated Peak Pro that heats, cools and saves profiles. Other demo modes act out connection problems: ?demo=unpaired (connection refused), ?demo=notpaired (no pairing), ?demo=pairing (pairing completes after a few tries) and ?demo=stuck (a read that never answers).

Do I need an account? Where are my profiles kept?

No account. The four profiles and the device settings live on the Peak Pro itself. My profiles, favorites and your °F/°C choice are saved in this browser only, so they don't follow you to another computer or browser. Use Back up on the Library tab before clearing site data or switching computers, and Restore to bring them back.

Does the site collect anything?

No. There are no analytics, no cookies and no server behind the page: it's static files on GitHub Pages, and the Bluetooth traffic goes straight from your browser to the device. Like any website, the host (GitHub) and the font service (Google Fonts) see the usual request details, such as your IP address.

Can I use it and the Puffco app at the same time?

No. The Peak Pro takes one connection at a time. Close the Puffco app (or turn off the phone's Bluetooth) before connecting here, and press Disconnect here before going back to the phone.

What happens if the page disconnects while heating?

The heat cycle runs on the device, so it carries on and ends on its own timer. The app shows Disconnected; press Connect to pick up again.

Which devices and firmware work?

The Peak Pro, on firmware AB or newer. Everything in the app has been confirmed on a real Peak Pro (firmware build 5652c02) from Chrome on Windows 11. Other Puffco devices haven't been tested.

Can I control it from Home Assistant?

Yes, with ha-puffco-peak, a HACS integration that runs the same device from Home Assistant over Bluetooth, locally. Only one of them can be connected at a time.

Is it safe?

It uses the same Bluetooth controls as the Puffco app and keeps every value within the device's limits. Nothing is permanent: no firmware is flashed and nothing is reset, and you can change any setting back here, on the device or in the Puffco app. Turning the device off asks first.

It's still a heater. Don't leave it heating unattended.

Found a bug?

Report a problem (also at the bottom of the app) opens a bug form on GitHub with the app version filled in. It helps to say whether the demo does the same thing, and to paste the Copy technical details text from a connection error or the Copy log text from the Protocol log tab. Posting needs a GitHub account.

Is this made by Puffco?

No. It's an independent, open-source project (GPL-3.0), not affiliated with Puffco. The Bluetooth protocol was worked out from my own device; the source credits the groundwork. Use it only with a device you own.
