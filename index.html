Control your Peak Pro from this page

Close the Puffco app on your phone first: the Peak Pro only accepts one Bluetooth connection at a time.

The first time you connect from this computer, put the device in pairing mode: hold the button until the logo glows blue. It powers off and back on before the blue light appears. Then press Connect.

    This browser can't use Bluetooth. Open the page in Chrome or Edge on a computer or Android phone.
